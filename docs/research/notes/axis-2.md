# Axis 2: products and prior art

Stand: 2026-11-01. Covered by report section 5.

Accepted evidence: S04-S13, S17-S24, S34, S36.

PICO Connect is a no-purchase baseline; Immersed explicitly supports real Mac virtual displays on Pico. Virtual Desktop release notes correct obsolete claims that Mac always needs external display software. Its original-Pico two-screen limit is a historical documented baseline, not a latest-version measurement.

PippinVR is unusually close to the goal but very young and Quest-targeted. Its APK route is worth adapting; its QuestLink route is not a Pico implementation. One human contributor, 13 stars and v1.2.0 are dated metadata, not quality certification. Other candidates contribute components, not a verified complete native-Mac/Pico answer. Do not count vendor pages as independent votes.
