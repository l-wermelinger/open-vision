# Axis 1: original Pico hardware and runtime

Stand: 2026-11-01. Covered by report sections 3-4, 6.2 and 7.

Accepted evidence: S01, S03, S25-S27, S31-S33.

2160x2160 per eye, XR2 Gen 1 and single colour-camera geometry constrain outcomes. Do not confuse original consumer Pico with Ultra/Pro/Enterprise. Eye tracking is absent on the assumed original device. New camera APIs exist, but original-device support was not established. Passthrough visibility does not establish access to camera pixels. OS 6 SDK examples do not establish original-Pico support.

Remaining evidence must come from exact SKU/firmware, runtime enumeration and a capability test. No device was tested.
